# Accounts Payable Policy (Northwind Ops)

1. Every vendor invoice must be entered in the billing system as a bill within 5 business days of receipt.
2. Bills of **USD 10,000 or more** must have the note `CFO approval required` so they are routed to the CFO.
3. Never enter the same invoice twice. Check the billing system for the invoice number first.
4. Vendor contact changes must be applied in the Vendors list as soon as the vendor notifies us.
5. Post a short note in the #finance chat channel when a bill over USD 10,000 is entered.
